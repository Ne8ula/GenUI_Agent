| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Detection-inspired | 0–7 s | Begin sparse on black: scattered anchored points and partial contours, not a completed façade. Four to six thin open-corner rectangles settle around real scene entities such as lamp, sign, table, people group and doorway. Bind each box to its entity's projected bounds and short noun; no labels on empty space, scores or telemetry. |
| 2. Density / segmentation-inspired | 7–14 s | Accumulate disconnected monochrome point/dither islands on object-specific surfaces. | 3. Authored-flow-inspired | 14–21 s | Visible particles travel along bounded object-local paths and settle at their destination anchors. Short directional dashes (roughly 8–24 screen pixels as a tunable target) show movement, particularly around figure routes; architecture does not drift. Keep marks away from faces/hands. No long spirals, full scan circles or claim of measured optical flow. |
| 4. Spatial-reconstruction-inspired | 21–30 s | Resolve partial front/back faces, short sections, local occlusion and selected material fragments. Layer perspective-aligned glyph fields, points and material detail on the **same surfaces**. Thin excessive construction trails while preserving meaningful gaps. Arrive at the selected composition without a connected full-road slab or closed enclosing room. |
