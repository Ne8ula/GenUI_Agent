# Authentic archive collection

Retrieved/checked 2026-10-14. **Three selected, actually retrieved and visually inspected archival assets: two photographs and one period document.** They are separate from generated concept media. This is a small curated subset about rail travel, street identification and public exhibitions, not a representative account of all Paris life in the 1980s. No file was uploaded to Weave; the first still uses source-supported text descriptions only. [Review](REVIEW.md).

### A03 — Petit Palais Rembrandt exhibition poster, 1986

- **Creator:** Gilles Huot, graphic designer. **Record:** [Paris Musées / Petit Palais](https://www.parismuseescollections.paris.fr/fr/petit-palais/oeuvres/rembrandt-eaux-fortes-exposition-paris-musee-du-petit-palais-6-fevrier-20-avril). The work is dated 1986 and announces the exhibition 6 February–20 April 1986 at the Petit Palais, Paris. Photomechanical poster, 60×40 cm. The supplied record does not expose a formal inventory number; `887121` is the download/record ID, not invented as an accession.
- [Official download bundle](https://www.parismuseescollections.paris.fr/fr/zip/oeuvre/887121); [IIIF manifest link](https://apicollections.parismusees.paris.fr/iiif/160053285/manifest) (link discovered, manifest not separately inspected).
- **Local image:** [archive/images/petit-palais-rembrandt-1986.jpg](archive/images/petit-palais-rembrandt-1986.jpg), unmodified official JPEG, 2047×3000, inspected with Read. Its dimensions differ from the physical 2:3 poster due to digitization/framing; no resolution claim inferred from the policy.
- **Bundled rights evidence:** [item credit text](archive/rights/cartel_huot_gilles_rembrandt._eaux__887121.txt) explicitly says “CC0 Paris Musées / Petit Palais, musée des Beaux-Arts de la Ville de Paris”; [three-page use guide](archive/rights/bonnesutilisations_opencontent_parismusees_fr_en_es.pdf), all three pages actually inspected, describes copying/modification/use including commercial use without requesting permission.
- **Live terms:** [Paris Musées terms](https://parismuseescollections.paris.fr/conditions-generales-d-utilisation); [CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). The live policy asks users to identify Paris Musées and link the collection site; retain title, author, museum and the supplied credit. Its guide also asks to keep the file open and not sell the file, and describes author moral rights. Those institutional statements are retained rather than silently equated with the legal CC0 text. The printed poster itself bears an older © Paris Musées inscription; record and downloaded credit both explicitly mark this digital asset CC0. This is source-declared reuse status, not independent legal clearance of every underlying element.
- **Credit line:** Gilles Huot, *Rembrandt. Eaux-fortes*, 1986, Petit Palais, musée des Beaux-Arts de la Ville de Paris. CC0 Paris Musées / Petit Palais. [Paris Musées collections](https://parismuseescollections.paris.fr/). Original image unchanged.
- **Visible:** red headings on pale paper; black etching reproduction of Rembrandt's face; readable exhibition title/place/dates. It is a genuine **1986 exhibition document**, not a photograph of a Paris resident in 1986. Do not turn the depicted seventeenth-century figure into a claim about 1980s fashion/people.
- **Supported annotation:** “Poster for the Petit Palais exhibition ‘Rembrandt. Eaux-fortes’, 6 February–20 April 1986.” The final procedural poster-shaped object is independently authored, not a reprint or counterfeit archive item; historical source windows may show the actual retained document under its stated terms.
