## Required experience, not yet implemented

1. **Request / desktop transition.** The intended runtime minimizes other applications and creates actual independent EVA desktop windows. Authoring uses only a generic synthetic desktop and synthetic application windows. Do not capture private desktop content or act on real windows now. Later scope must explicitly cover effect authorization, native ownership, focus, interruption, restore-if-unchanged and crash recovery. A generated video proves none of these functions. No camera, voice or wallpaper operation is authorized by this pass.
2. **Curated authentic sources.** Small particles first form the borders of several overlapping source windows; those windows present real photographs and documents dated 1980–1989 and located in Paris. Label the sequence as a curated archive, not live retrieval. Each item needs creator, source URL, date/location evidence, rights/reuse status and supported facts. Black/white dithered borders and headers replace the blue bars in the owner's reference. Rectangles attach to source-supported details with short explanations; preserve each object→claim→source path. No random boxes or invented confidence values.
3. **Windows become particles.** Regions of the source windows break into particles that travel into emerging objects; visible particle trajectories connect departure and arrival. Do not fade out whole windows to reveal a finished background. This visual transfer does not authorize photo-derived pixels in the final Rust objects.
4. **Continuous construction, 30–45 seconds.** All four stages below use the same black/white dither vocabulary. Detection, segmentation, optical flow and spatial reconstruction are explicitly visualization inspirations, not claims that computer-vision models ran. No ML/inference infrastructure is authorized.
5. **Finished objects / inspection.** Separate recognizable objects occupy desktop space with gaps, alongside partial city elements whose local relationships are readable. No single first-person view or enclosed corridor. Broken contours and incomplete surfaces remain after construction. Hover gives short identity plus an object-attached rectangle; clicking pins historical explanation and source links. Inspection modestly increases the relevant object's density, not photorealism or fully filled surfaces. Show hover and click in a later video study. Wheel navigation remains unspecified.
6. **Later requests.** The user can ask to revise the scene. Proposed rule: patch affected objects and preserve unaffected objects, IDs and pins; detailed behavior is a proposal, not an owner-approved interaction specification.

## Timing proposal for a 30-second construction clip

A 30-second clip fits the discovered Seedance 2.5 contract. These subdivisions are authoring proposals, not owner decisions or measured processing times. Separate desktop, source-transfer and final-inspection clips are outside these 30 seconds.

| Time | Visible instruction | Invariants |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–7 s: detection-inspired | 4–6 thin rectangles settle around object candidates; one short noun per rectangle. A rectangle moves with its assigned object and never labels empty space. | Fixed object IDs, desktop coordinates and projection; no scores or fabricated detection output. |
| 7–14 s: segmentation-inspired | Alternate black/white point densities separate a person's coat from background, a vehicle body from its wheels and a structure from its supports. Silhouette regions remain disconnected where source parts are missing. | No rainbow masks, glow, opaque filled scenery or new palette. |
| 14–21 s: optical-flow-inspired | Short 8–24 px trails follow particles moving along each object's shape or route. Use denser trajectories only on the moving figure/vehicle; stationary structures remain anchored. | Trails describe authored movement, not measured historical trajectories. No camera orbit required. |
| 21–30 s: spatial-reconstruction-inspired | Points establish front/back faces, short cross-sections and selected partial surfaces. Object-relative occlusion makes depth readable. Remove excess construction trails while preserving contour gaps. | No floor plane joining every object, no closing street walls, no complete surfaced environment. |

Later inspection proposal: increase the hovered object's local point coverage by roughly 10–15 percentage points over 0.4–0.6 s while leaving its pose/scale and other objects unchanged; retain at least one major surface gap. These are tunable design targets, not measured output or accepted values. Provide a static reduced-motion equivalent when implementation is scoped.
