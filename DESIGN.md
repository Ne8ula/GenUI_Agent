## Experience and continuity

EVA's eye accompanies the encounter. The remembered room forms through deliberate spatial composition, quiet moments and readable transitions. Its structure and materials should carry the artistic merit.

Retain the eye identity, square pupil/dithering and attention cues. Explore Week 4's sparse points, contours, beaded strands, incomplete surfaces and localized material changes. These are a vocabulary, not proof of ML processing or a requirement to reproduce Paris.

## Interaction

- Preserve IDs, source links, annotation anchors, locks, selection and camera context across revisions.
- Support navigation on actual spatial structure; bound it honestly when coverage is insufficient.
- Object expansion preserves its room position and gives a clear return.
- Hover also works through keyboard focus and touch. Escape/dismissal clears stale information and invisible hit targets.
- Interruption, pause, intensity and reduced motion stay local. Reduced motion provides a stable representative state.
- Keep source/provenance detail available without turning the encounter into a dashboard.

## Representation honesty

Edge filters are not detected geometry; authored boxes are not detection; scan-like points are not LiDAR; Gaussian sprites are not trained 3DGS. Generated completion and user memory remain distinguishable from supplied evidence.

Construction may express real structure, but animation timing does not measure algorithm progress, confidence or historical truth. Keep raw reconstruction inspectable; expose unsupported regions.

Trust/error states use explicit words, shape and placement, not color alone. Runtime styling cannot reach safety-owned confirmations. Runtime models supply bounded data, never executable HTML/JS/CSS/shaders, shell or IPC.

## Authoring gate
