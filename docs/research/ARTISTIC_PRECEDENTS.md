## Sunshine: mediated intensity

Reference: *Sunshine* (2007), directed by Danny Boyle, written by Alex Garland. The owner recalls an AI-mediated encounter with intense sunlight at approximately “31% effectiveness.” **That numerical detail and wording remain owner recollection, not verified dialogue or an engineering parameter.** A primary film clip or screenplay passage establishing the exact number was not verified here. Do not substitute a number from a community transcript.

Use the owner's reference as a design brief: anticipation → explicit adjustment of mediation → a change in apparent scale and presence → recovery. This is an **EVA interpretation**, not a shot-by-shot analysis of a watched scene. On a desktop, intensity can change occupied area, density, contrast within a fixed cap, or the relation between stillness and movement. The user controls the reveal and can reverse it. Maximum luminance, strobing, automatic screen-brightness changes, and prolonged forced spectacle are unnecessary.

For the weather example, the answer appears first. An optional expansion lets the sun occupy more of the composition while text stays stationary; a passing cloud changes occlusion, and a quiet setting settles the image. The emotional hypothesis concerns mediation and proportion, not retinal intensity or a universal association of sunshine with happiness.

## Design consequences to test

1. **Spatial attachment:** preserve explicit referents during transformation; compare with floating decoration that has no stable target.
2. **Emergence:** let a small vocabulary assemble differently with the subject and intervention; record what determined each change.
3. **Materiality:** use a consistent pixel/dither field with crisp facts. Texture is not a source of data or trust.
4. **Gesture:** explicit local manipulation is sufficient; no camera prerequisite. A gesture can change a composition without authorizing external access.
5. **Continuity:** the current response becomes the material for the next one. User placement, selection, and refusal survive the revision.
6. **Temporal invitation:** allow the user to linger, skip, simplify, or close. Artful waiting cannot be used to conceal avoidable system latency.

These consequences are design hypotheses derived from precedent analysis. The [next experiment](../design/experiments/E1_REVISABLE_WEATHER.md) tests them as response behavior rather than claiming that the references have already demonstrated EVA's intended effects.
